What stays consistent

Highs lead at 18pt; lows recede at 14pt. UV stays below. Rain appears above 0.05″; wind appears above 15 mph. Temperature labels have no background or connector. Competition swimmers sit in solid teal discs directly on beaches.

The blue-to-red field represents daily highs, not elevation. It interpolates illustrative forecasts; it is not live weather. Gradient-only maps use five temperature labels and retain qualifying wind at the overview locations. Parks and hills remain separate layers. Textured hills use slope-aligned hachures; tree texture repeats only inside parks.

All 11 overview points are rendered at every width in reading modes. Small previews scale the whole map; use “Inspect larger” to judge the selected 18pt / 14pt typography at full size. Park, bridge and road names remain off the map.
