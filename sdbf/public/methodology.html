How Beach Finder scores water quality

The safety score runs from 1 to 10, with 1 for the lowest observed concern and 10 for dangerous conditions. It describes the evidence available for the named monitoring point. Conditions can change between samples and along the beach.

ScoreMeaning
1–2Lowest observed concern
3–4Use caution
5–6Sensitive groups should avoid
7–8Avoid water contact
9–10Dangerous. Avoid water contact.

What raises the score

  • County postings and verified spills. A County closure or warning, or a verified spill affecting the site, scores 10. A water-quality advisory scores at least 9. A confirmed rain-only advisory uses the rain scale. The published estimate of a lift stays informational until the County updates its postings.
  • Rain and runoff. Crossing the local runoff limit of 0.20 inches or the four-day inland limit of 0.75 inches sets a score of at least 6. Local totals of 0.35 inches or inland totals of 1.00 inch set 7. Heavy rain of at least 0.50 inches locally or 1.25 inches inland can score 8 by itself. Qualifying coastal rain or a Countywide rain advisory sets a coastwide minimum of 7 during the event and through the 72-hour hold. The inland thresholds and the 1–10 scale are app rules.
  • Bacteria and sample age. A result at or above the method’s limit in the past 72 hours scores at least 9. A result at twice the limit, or a recent exceedance with qualifying local rainfall, scores 10. An exceedance older than 72 hours can only raise an otherwise clear score from 1 to 2. An older below-limit result or an unavailable sample adds no points. A recent result near the limit can increase the score.
  • Missing evidence. One missing or stale check raises the minimum to 5. Multiple gaps raise it to 6. This applies to County status, coastwide rain status, and local and inland rain coverage. Sample availability and age appear alongside the score.

With current County and rainfall information and no negative indicators, the score starts at 1. The strongest signal sets the score. A low reading, a dry gauge, or a County open status cannot cancel another hazard. Enterococcus limits are 1,413 DNA copies per 100 mL for ddPCR and 104 organisms per 100 mL for culture. Reported bounds such as <195 retain their original qualifier.

Rainfall and runoff

Local rain covers the last 72 hours. Inland rain covers the last 96 hours at a point about three miles east of each beach station. Every beach uses the same rainfall rules because runoff can reach the coast through creeks, lagoons, drains, and other pathways.

Rainfall comes from Open-Meteo’s coordinate series, with NWS gauges supplying additional coastal readings. Forecast hours appear on the runoff chart and stay outside the current rating.
