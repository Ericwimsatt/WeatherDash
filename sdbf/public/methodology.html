How Beach Finder rates water quality

  • Avoid water contact. A County closure, warning or advisory, a reported spill affecting the site, or the latest comparable bacteria result at or above its limit takes priority.
  • Avoid after rain. A County general rain advisory or evidence of qualifying rain applies coastwide for 72 hours after the rain ends. A tentative lift time stays informational until the County confirms the lift.
  • Insufficient data. The site has missing or stale evidence for the County posting, rain, runoff or a comparable bacteria result.
  • Recent test below limit. The County shows the site open, rain and runoff checks are current, and a comparable result collected in the past 48 hours is below its limit and after any known hazard. A sample describes its monitoring point and collection time.

Enterococcus limits are 1,413 DNA copies per 100 mL for ddPCR and 104 organisms per 100 mL for culture. The app keeps reported bounds such as <195 and distinguishes measured rain totals from minimum totals when gauge observations are incomplete. A mapped outfall indicates a possible source of runoff. Its mapped location alone cannot tell you whether water is flowing today.
